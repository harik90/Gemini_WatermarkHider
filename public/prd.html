01

Overview & Problem Statement

Google's frontier image and video generation models (Gemini, Nano Banana, Veo, Flow) composite a visible four-point sparkle watermark at fixed positions on every exported output. Creators, marketing teams, designers, and agencies utilizing these models for client presentations, marketing collateral, and production creative often require clean assets unencumbered by this mark.

ClearMark is designed as a focused creative utility that removes this watermark from images and videos in bulk, instantly, and to a pixel-perfect standard. Rather than masquerading as a generic ad-supported online tool, ClearMark maintains an intentional, restrained experience that prioritizes privacy, batch throughput, and mathematical fidelity.

02

Core Mathematical Insight

The Gemini watermark is not stamped as opaque pixels, nor is it an unconstrained inpainting challenge. It is composited using standard linear alpha blending with a fixed astroid star shape and known opacity map (α) at calibrated resolution steps:

Forward Composition
watermarked_pixel = α × 255 + (1 − α) × original_pixel

Because the logo color is fixed white (255) and α is pre-calibrated, the forward blend is algebraically invertible.

Exact Algebraic Inverse
original_pixel = (watermarked_pixel − α × 255) / (1 − α)

Reversal is deterministic and runs entirely on device via the Canvas API. No server-side inference or GPU roundtrips are required.

Boundary Robustness: In low-luminance regions (e.g. night skies or dark water), aggressive alpha inversion can risk underflow artifacts. ClearMark enforces an alpha safety ceiling (α ≤ 0.38), local 5×5 neighborhood sanity checks, and a bilateral boundary inpainting fallback to ensure zero dark blotches occur under any lighting condition.

03

Goals & Scope Boundaries

Clear boundaries ensure the product ships with depth on its core thesis rather than dilute quality across tangential workflows.

Primary Goals

  • Lossless restoration: Zero visible blur or boundary ringing for Gemini and Veo watermarks on supported resolutions.
  • True bulk capacity: Process dozens to hundreds of files in parallel via Web Workers without degrading browser responsiveness.
  • Frame-by-frame video: Support MP4, MOV, and WebM video files with original audio streams preserved and zero quality loss.
  • Local privacy: Zero file uploads to external servers. All image and video processing executes inside the user's browser sandbox.

Out of Scope for Version 1.0

  • Removing arbitrary third-party logos or opaque watermarks via generative diffusion inpainting.
  • Native mobile applications (iOS/Android) — v1 is an optimized, responsive web application.
  • Multiplayer team workspaces, cloud project storage, or enterprise asset management.
  • Circumventing steganographic watermarks (e.g. SynthID), which are structurally distinct and not visually intrusive.

05

Feature Specifications

Upload & Ingestion Pipeline

  • Multi-file selection via file picker, paste from clipboard (Cmd/Ctrl+V), and drag-and-drop.
  • Recursive folder ingestion via webkitGetAsEntry to traverse nested asset directories.
  • In-browser ZIP decompression using JSZip to immediately expand archives into queue items without server uploads.

Detection & Positioning Engine

  • Two-pass coarse-to-fine 2D cross-correlation scan across bottom-right and corner quadrants.
  • Correlation peak estimation against calibrated 32px, 48px, 64px, and 96px astroid alpha templates.
  • Confidence scoring (0.0 to 1.0) surfacing review flags only when confidence falls below 0.45.

Reverse Blend & Inpaint Hybrid

  • Primary reverse alpha blending with dynamic feathering via smoothstep radial decay.
  • Local 5×5 neighborhood sanity verification discarding overcorrections that fall >40 luminance units darker than surrounding context.
  • Boundary Laplace diffusion fallback providing seamless fill for uncalibrated watermark regions.

07

Success Metrics & Polish

  • Time-to-first-clean-result: Under 3.0 seconds from image drop to visible clean preview on standard hardware.
  • High-confidence auto-detection: Greater than 95% detection accuracy on unmodified Gemini/Veo exports without manual intervention.
  • Visual artifact threshold: Zero solid black blotches, zero edge halo lines, and seamless texture continuity on dark landscapes and night shots.
  • Batch completion rate: Greater than 92% of initiated batches successfully exported as individual files or ZIP archives.
08

Open Questions & Decisions

  • Nano Banana / Flow calibration: Evaluate whether secondary model releases introduce distinct alpha masks or remain identical to Gemini's 48px/64px profiles.
  • Browser extension companion: Assess demand for an extension enabling one-click download directly inside Google Workspace and Gemini web interfaces.
  • Batch size gates: Establish tier limits for single-session batch volume (e.g. 25 images on free tier vs. unlimited on paid tier).
